# Property Company

A real estate brokerage with residential and commercial sales, a property management desk, marketing and administration.

## The problem
Agents work from WhatsApp and personal phones. Nobody can see who handles which listing or client, files live on laptops, and leads get lost between channels.

## Company structure
- Administrative: contracts, commissions, invoices, HR
- Sales: Residential and Commercial
- Property Management: tenancies, renewals, maintenance
- Marketing: listing photos, ads, campaigns
